ThinAir provides a unified fleet management platform that combines real‑time vehicle tracking, driver safety, dispatch routing, ELD/HOS compliance, asset tracking, maintenance, and analytics. Their solutions include ThinAir Fleet, Thinair ELD, ThinAir Geo, and ThinAir Data, delivering live operational visibility for trucking, construction, oil & gas, field service, utilities, and waste‑recycling industries. The platform offers APIs and MCP servers for developers to integrate location intelligence, operational data, and compliance services into their own applications.

**Areas:** Company · fleet-management · telematics · logistics · compliance · API
